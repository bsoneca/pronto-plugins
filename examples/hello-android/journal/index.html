Turn 4 — a real pronto app

The Gradle hello world became a pronto app: bootstrapped on pronto 0.14.0 and sayt 0.44.1, with brief, acceptance, ir, DESIGN and program.cue cut from pronto's idioms to the counter. The Android project moved into android/, its package renamed to dev.pronto.hello_android, the id the Android host derives from the app's name. The phone now reads the program's own shell/handlers/count.js and shell/shell.json seed instead of copies; its @android verbs and toolchain come from bsoneca/pronto-plugins.

What is not yet pronto's

  • The phone's screen is drawn by android/app/src/main/assets/screen.js, not compiled from shell/screens/counter.html; pronto 0.14.0 has no android target.
  • The engine is a cut-down HelloEngine, not omnishell's interpreter; the row lives in memory and resets on restart.
  • SES is a vendored copy of omnishell's ses.umd.min.js 1.15.0.
  • The web screenshots came from headless Chrome told to accept the local mkcert certificate, which the Mac does not trust.

Turn 3 — the toolchain as a plugin

The JDK, the SDK's cmdline-tools and the setup, build, test and launch rules moved out of the app into the Android host's declaration, now bsoneca/pronto-plugins. The app holds what it generates: a .say.json sayt merges with pronto's .say.yaml, and a mise drop-in. ./saytw launch@android built, installed and started the app from it, exit 0.

Turn 2 — pinned toolchain and sayt verbs

Homebrew's global JDK and SDK gave way to mise pins (Temurin 21.0.12+8, android-sdk 23.0, locked), Gradle's wrapper got its checksum, and ./saytw setup and ./saytw launch@android replaced raw gradlew and adb; both exited 0.

Turn 1 — a native hello world

pronto's idioms count.js, unchanged, ran in QuickJS under SES on the phone and drew a DivKit card; taps came back as pronto://event URLs. The first build drew black text on the phone's dark background, left-aligned.

Measured along the way: SES needs a host console and an Iterator.prototype repair on QuickJS 2026-06-04; quickjs-kt needs scripts to end in undefined; omnishell's jessie.js cannot load yet, for want of URL, TextEncoder, Intl and the other web globals.
