2026-10-18 · watching it: a local dashboard and the failure switch

sayt launch@journeys now starts VictoriaMetrics, vmalert and Grafana beside the collector, fed the generated rules and dashboard, and prints the app, Grafana and alert addresses. The counter gained a Fail button: the same count handler, made to throw.

Found by watching it, fixed

  • The terminal moves between screens without unloading the page, so a batch waiting for 20 events or a pagehide could sit in the tab indefinitely. The reporter now also flushes 2 s after a batch's first event.
  • Chrome dropped the batch sent at pagehide when the page reloaded its own address. Three Fail presses with reloads counted 1. What a page holds as it goes is now kept in the tab and sent by the next load, and the collector's dedupe drops any repeat: 3 of 3.
  • Each outcome's counter is born at its first run, and rate() over a series that has not moved since is 0, so a fresh journey read 0/0: "No data". The rules and the dashboard now take ratios of increase(), which VictoriaMetrics counts from a series' first sample.
  • Pressing Fail again on a screen already in network-error counted as ok: the terminal writes the state again, but the reporter only read changes. It now settles on every write: five presses on one screen, five failures. The local stack no longer holds back the newest 30 s (VictoriaMetrics' latency offset) or backdates evaluations by 30 s (vmalert's eval delay), so a press shows within about 40 s.
